Your bank & UPI SMS, turned into a private ledger.

SpendSense reads the transaction messages your bank already sends you, turns them into a searchable ledger with budgets and insights, and keeps all of it encrypted on your phone. No bank login. No account required.

Download the latest APK

What it does

  • Reads transaction SMS — debit and credit messages from your bank and UPI apps are parsed on the device.
  • Keeps the ledger encrypted — AES-256-GCM on the device, with the key in the Android Keystore.
  • Budgets and alerts — monthly cycle, per-category limits, warning thresholds.
  • Bills and recurring plans — detects repeat payments and tracks what is coming.
  • Insights — category breakdown, seven-day activity, month-over-month comparison.
  • Manual entries and export — add anything by hand and export everything as JSON.

What leaves your phone

Nothing, unless you switch it on. Parsing happens on the device, the transactions stay there, and the app has no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDK. Optional cloud features — an account, AI-assisted parsing, the assistant, and Gmail import — are each off by default and each explained before you enable it.

Install

  1. Open the latest release.
  2. Download the .apk.
  3. Allow installs from your browser or file manager when prompted.
  4. Install. Requires Android 7.0 or newer.

Updating over an existing build with the same signing key keeps your data.
