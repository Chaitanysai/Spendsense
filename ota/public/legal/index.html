Legal & privacy

SpendSense is an offline-first expense tracker for Android. This page covers what the app stores, what leaves your device, and the terms you agree to by using it.

The short version. Your transactions are stored on your phone in an encrypted database. We have no server that holds your ledger. Nothing is sent to an AI provider unless you turn it on yourself.

Privacy Policy

Who we are

SpendSense is an independent app published by Chaitanya Sai Kandula, an individual developer based in India. Contact: chaitanyasaikandula@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Transactions, budgets, bills, recurring plans and insights. Stored in a local SQLite database, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key lives in the platform's secure storage (Android Keystore), not in the database and not on any server.
  • Your SMS inbox. SpendSense reads bank and UPI messages only when you grant SMS permission. Parsing happens on the phone.
  • Your profile photo and display name. Stored locally.
  • App settings and theme. Stored in Android SharedPreferences.

What leaves your device, and only if you turn it on

1. Account (optional)

Signing in is optional. If you create an account we store your email address and display name with Google Firebase Authentication, and a profile document (display name, email, timestamps) in Google Cloud Firestore. Signing in also enables optional multi-device sync of your transaction rows; those rows are stored per-account in Firestore and readable only by that account.

The financial ledger is not stored in the cloud for backup. Sync exists so a second device sees the same rows; it is not a backup service and we do not restore from it on your behalf.

2. Cloud SMS parsing (off by default)

SpendSense parses SMS on the device by default. If a message is ambiguous and you have enabled Settings → AI Provider Settings → Send ambiguous SMS to AI providers, the message text is redacted and sent to the provider you configured (Groq, Cerebras, Google Gemini, or OpenRouter) to extract amount, merchant and category. That provider processes the text under its own terms. Leave this off and no SMS text leaves the phone.

3. SpendSense Assistant (off by default)

The assistant runs locally by default. If you explicitly enable cloud assistance, your question and the minimum relevant spending figures are sent through our Firebase Cloud Function proxy to Google Gemini. The proxy holds the API key; your device never does. You can revoke consent and clear the audit log at any time in Settings → Assistant privacy & Gemini.

4. Email import (off by default)

If you enable email import, SpendSense requests read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) to find bank alert emails. It reads; it never sends, modifies or deletes mail. You can revoke this at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

5. Google Sign-In and profile photo

If you sign in with Google, Google authenticates you and returns your email, name and profile photo to the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data. Ever, to anyone.
  • We do not run advertising or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. There is no Firebase Analytics, no Crashlytics and no equivalent in the app.
  • We do not ask for your bank credentials, card numbers or UPI PIN. The app reads messages your bank already sent you.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos beyond the one you choose, location, or device advertising ID.

Permissions and why

  • Read SMS — to find bank and UPI transaction messages.
  • Notifications — budget alerts and update prompts.
  • Internet — optional sign-in, sync, AI features and app updates.
  • Photos / media — only the single image you pick for your profile.

Declining any permission leaves the rest of the app working.

How long we keep things

  • On your device: until you delete it. Removing a transaction deletes it; clearing the database deletes everything; uninstalling the app removes the local database.
  • Cloud sync rows: tombstones are retained so a device that was offline cannot resurrect a deleted row, then pruned. Deleting your account deletes your profile document and your synced rows.
  • Assistant audit log: stored on your device, cleared by you.

Your rights

You can access and correct your data in the app, export everything as JSON (Settings → Export JSON backup), delete individual records, and delete your entire account in the app. To make a request you cannot complete in the app — including a copy of what we hold, its correction, or its erasure — email chaitanyasaikandula@gmail.com with the subject "Data request". We respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA or UK you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 applies.

Children

SpendSense is not directed at children under 13 (or under 18 where local law sets that threshold for financial services). We do not knowingly collect data from a child. If you believe a child created an account, email us and we will delete it. An account requires an email address and a password, and no in-app purchase or advertising targets minors.

Security

The local database is encrypted at rest. Cloud traffic uses TLS. Our assistant proxy verifies requests with Firebase App Check and enforces per-device and per-account rate limits. No system is perfectly secure; if we discover a breach affecting your data we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

International transfers

Firebase (Google) and the AI providers process data in data centres outside India. Where information leaves your country, the transfer relies on the provider's standard contractual clauses. If you do not sign in and do not enable AI features, no data is transferred at all.

Delete your account and data

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open SpendSense and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your profile and synced rows are deleted and you are signed out.

Local ledger data stays on your device until you remove it: Settings → Clear transaction database, or uninstall the app.

By email

If you cannot sign in, email chaitanyasaikandula@gmail.com from the address on the account, with the subject "Delete my SpendSense account". We verify and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted, and what is not

  • Deleted: your Firebase Authentication account, your Firestore profile document, and your synced transaction rows.
  • Not deleted by us: the encrypted ledger on your phone, which never reached us. Clear it in the app or uninstall.
  • Retained: records we are legally required to keep, if any. We currently keep none.
